# Getting started with KindPath (for charity administrators)

This is the guide for the person setting KindPath up for a temple, church, mosque
or charity. No technical background needed. It takes about ten minutes plus
however long WeVend takes to issue your merchant account.

## 1. Create your account

Go to **kind-path.org** and choose **Start free trial**. Enter your
organization's name, your name, your work email and a password.

You'll get a confirmation email — click the link when you see it. You can keep
going without it; we just like to know the address is real because that's where
receipts and billing notices go.

## 2. Set up your organization (four short steps)

You're taken straight into setup. You can leave and come back — the dashboard
will show a **Continue setup** button until you finish.

### Step 1 — Organization & receipts

- **Are you a CRA-registered charity?** If yes, enter your registration number
  (it looks like `123456789 RR 0001`) and the name of the person who signs
  receipts. Only registered charities can issue official donation receipts;
  everyone else sends donors a payment confirmation instead.
- **Address.** It appears on every receipt — the CRA requires it.
- Pick your province from the list and enter your postal code.

### Step 2 — Your branding (optional)

A brand colour, a logo link and a thank-you line for receipts. Skip it if you
like; all of it lives in **Settings** later.

### Step 3 — Pick your plan

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial and you aren't charged until it ends.
Monthly or annual (annual is two months free). You can change plans any time.

### Step 4 — Get paid

This is the step that matters. KindPath never holds your money; donations go
straight to **your** WeVend merchant account. Until you connect one, your giving
page still works but the money does not reach you — and the dashboard will keep
reminding you.

**Don't have a WeVend merchant account?** Ask KindPath support — we'll have
WeVend set one up for your organization. You'll get a claim email from WeVend
with a registration code; follow it, set your password, and you'll then have a
**Merchant ID (MID)** and a **Terminal ID** (usually `00000003`).

**Connect it:** enter the merchant ID and terminal ID, then press **Connect
account**. We check the merchant ID with WeVend before saving.

**We never ask for your WeVend password.** Your WeVend account stays yours;
KindPath bills through it as your provider. (If your merchant account isn't set
up under KindPath, the form will ask for your WeVend login as well — that's the
exception, not the norm.)

**Refunds — please read.** KindPath does not issue refunds yet. To refund a
donor: refund the payment in your **WeVend merchant portal**, then come back to
KindPath and **void that donor's receipt** (Receipts → the receipt → Void).
Both steps matter — the CRA does not allow a valid receipt to stand for money
that was given back, and KindPath cannot see refunds made in WeVend.

Press **Finish setup**. (If you really must, **Skip for now** finishes without a
gateway — you'll be told clearly that donations won't reach you yet.)

## 3. Your giving page and QR code

The "You're set up" screen shows your public giving link
(`kind-path.org/give/your-name`) and a QR code. Copy the link for your website,
emails and socials; download the QR for pews, entrances and bulletins. Both are
always available under **Giving page & QR** in the dashboard.

## 4. Make a test donation

Open your giving page and give a small amount. You'll be taken to WeVend's secure
card page and back. A real card is charged — refund it from KindPath afterwards
(if KindPath tells you the platform is in WeVend's sandbox, use the test card
numbers WeVend gave you instead). Within a minute the donor address you used receives a receipt email
(an official donation receipt if you're registered, a payment confirmation if
not), and the gift appears under **Donations** and **Receipts**.

## 5. Day to day

- **Donors** — everyone who has given; their receipts, recurring plans, consent.
- **Receipts** — every receipt issued; re-send, or void-and-reissue (issued receipts
  are never edited, the CRA requires that).
- **Recurring plans** — monthly givers; failed payments are retried and you're told.
- **Communications** — receipts that didn't deliver, and campaigns.
- **Settings** — payments, branding, receipt details, receipt numbering.
- **Team** — invite staff; staff can do most things but cannot change where money
  goes.
- **Security** — turn on two-factor authentication for your own account. Please do.

Donors can sign in to their own portal with a 6-digit emailed code — no password
to set up — to download receipts and manage recurring gifts.

## 6. Changing things later

Everything from setup lives in **Settings**. Connecting, replacing or disconnecting
your WeVend merchant account is **Settings → Payments**.

## Need help?

Use the contact form on kind-path.org. When you write, say what you were doing and
the time — everything that changes money or receipts is logged so we can see
exactly what happened.
